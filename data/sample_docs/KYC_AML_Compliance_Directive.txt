## 1. REGULATORY FRAMEWORK
This master directive enforces compliance with the Prevention of Money Laundering Act (PMLA), Financial Action Task Force (FATF) Recommendations, and Reserve Bank / Central Banking KYC Master Directions.

## 2. CUSTOMER DUE DILIGENCE (CDD) CATEGORIES
Section 2.1 Low Risk Customers:
- Salaried individuals with clear source of funds, government employees, and pensioners.
- Re-KYC Frequency: Once every 10 years.
- Simplified KYC via e-KYC (OTP/Biometric) with PAN verification.

Section 2.2 Medium Risk Customers:
- Small merchants, sole proprietorships, trusts, and individuals whose transaction volumes exceed normal baseline.
- Re-KYC Frequency: Once every 8 years.

Section 2.3 High Risk Customers:
- Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs) and their immediate family members / close associates.
- Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) dealing in offshore remittance jurisdictions.
- Entities with complex beneficial ownership structures or shell company indicators.
- Re-KYC Frequency: Mandatory updated documentation every 2 years.
- Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD) and Principal Officer sign-off required prior to account activation.

## 3. BENEFICIAL OWNERSHIP (BO) IDENTIFICATION
1. Corporate Entities: Any natural person who exercises control through ownership of or is entitled to more than 10% of shares, capital, or profits of the juridical person.
2. Unincorporated Associations / Trusts: Any natural person holding more than 15% of the property or capital interest.
3. If no natural person is identifiable, the Senior Managing Official (SMO) must be recorded as the de-facto beneficial owner.

## 4. TRANSACTION MONITORING & SUSPICIOUS TRANSACTION REPORTING (STR)
Section 4.1 Automated Rule Alerts:
- Cash Transaction Report (CTR): All cash deposits or withdrawals exceeding $10,000 (INR 10,00,000) or equivalent in a calendar month must be systematically logged and reported to the FIU within 15 days.
- Structuring / Smurfing: Multiple cash deposits just below the $10,000 threshold across linked accounts within 7 business days trigger immediate Level-2 AML escalation.
- Cross-Border Inward Remittances: Any single wire transfer exceeding $25,000 from FATF Gray/Black-listed countries requires source-of-wealth documentation before credit release.

Section 4.2 Suspicious Transaction Reporting (STR) Submission:
- The Principal Compliance Officer must furnish the STR to Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) within 7 working days of arriving at a conclusion of suspicion.
- Tipping Off Prohibition: Bank personnel are strictly forbidden under federal statute from disclosing or hinting to the customer that an STR or AML investigation has been initiated.

## 5. DORMANT ACCOUNT REACTIVATION SOP
- Accounts with no customer-induced transactions for 24 continuous months are flagged as INOPERATIVE/DORMANT.
- Reactivation requires in-person branch presence, fresh self-attested KYC documents, biometric/signature verification, and a test transaction of nominal amount. No fees may be charged for reactivation.
