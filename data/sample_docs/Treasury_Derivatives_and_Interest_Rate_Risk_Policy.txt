## 1. PERMISSIBLE TREASURY & DERIVATIVE INSTRUMENTS
Section 1.1 Authorized Products:
Apex Global Bank trades and hedges interest rate and forex risk using approved instruments:
1. Rupee Interest Rate Swaps (IRS): Overnight Indexed Swaps (OIS) referenced against MIBOR.
2. Forward Rate Agreements (FRA) and Cross-Currency Interest Rate Swaps (CCIRS).
3. Plain Vanilla Foreign Currency Options (Call/Put) and USD/INR Currency Futures.
4. Structured derivatives with leveraged payoffs or dual-currency trigger caps are strictly prohibited for non-institutional retail clients.

## 2. MARKET RISK LIMITS & VALUE AT RISK (VaR)
Section 2.1 Value at Risk (VaR) Benchmark:
- 1-Day Holding Period Value at Risk (VaR) is computed at the 99.0% statistical confidence level using historical simulation across 250 trading days.
- Aggregate Bank Treasury VaR Limit: Capped at $2,500,000 across all asset classes.
- PV01 (Price Value of a Basis Point) Limit: Maximum portfolio sensitivity of $75,000 per 1 basis point shift in the sovereign yield curve.

Section 2.2 Net Open Overnight Position Limit (NOOPL):
- Maximum overnight foreign exchange open position is capped at $5,000,000 USD equivalent.
- Intraday Daylight Limit: Capped at $12,000,000 USD equivalent.
- Stop-Loss Breach Protocol: If daily trading draw-down exceeds $250,000, all open trading desks must halt new position opening and notify the Head of Treasury Risk immediately.
