## 1. SAVINGS ACCOUNT VARIANTS & MINIMUM BALANCES
Section 1.1 Classic Savings Account:
- Minimum Average Monthly Balance (AMB):
  - Metro / Urban Branches: $150 (INR 10,000)
  - Semi-Urban / Rural Branches: $75 (INR 5,000)
- Non-Maintenance Fee: $5.00 / month (or INR 250 + GST).
- Interest Rate: 3.50% p.a. calculated on daily end-of-day balances and credited quarterly.
- Complimentary Debit Card: Classic RuPay / Visa with daily ATM withdrawal limit of $500 (INR 40,000).

Section 1.2 Privilege Gold Savings Account:
- Minimum AMB: $750 (INR 50,000) or total relationship value of $5,000.
- Zero non-maintenance penalty if linked with active recurring deposit >= $100/mo.
- Free unlimited ATM transactions across all domestic non-Apex bank ATMs.
- Platinum International Debit Card with $2,000 daily spend limit and zero fuel surcharge.

Section 1.3 Senior Citizen Special Savings:
- Eligibility: Resident individuals aged 60 years and above.
- Zero minimum balance requirement with quarterly free doorstep banking visit.
- Additional 0.25% interest rate on savings balances exceeding $1,500.

## 2. TERM DEPOSITS / FIXED DEPOSIT (FD) RATE MATRIX
Section 2.1 Standard Fixed Deposit Rates (Tenure vs Yield):
- 7 Days to 45 Days: 4.50% p.a.
- 46 Days to 179 Days: 5.75% p.a.
- 180 Days to 364 Days: 6.50% p.a.
- 1 Year to Less Than 2 Years: 7.10% p.a.
- 2 Years Special 'Apex Prosperity' (444 Days): 7.45% p.a. (Highest Retail Yield)
- 3 Years to 5 Years: 6.80% p.a.
- 5 Years Tax Saver FD (Section 80C Lock-in 5 Years): 7.00% p.a.

Section 2.2 Senior Citizen Premium:
- Additional 0.50% p.a. on all tenures above 1 year (yielding up to 7.95% on 444-day deposits).

Section 2.3 Premature Withdrawal Terms:
- Permitted on all regular deposits.
- Penalty: A deduction of 0.50% interest rate from the applicable card rate for the duration the deposit was actually held.
- Tax Saver FDs (5-year lock-in) cannot be closed prematurely under statutory tax guidelines.

## 3. DIGITAL BANKING SUPPORT & DEBIT CARD RE-ISSUANCE
- Lost / Stolen Card Instant Hotlisting: Available 24/7 via Mobile Banking App, SMS to 5676767, or IVR Helpline 1800-APEX-BANK.
- Replacement Card Fee: $2.50 (INR 150) for Classic; Free for Privilege Gold and Platinum members.
- Daily UPI Transaction Cap: $1,200 (INR 1,00,000) per 24-hour cycle per central payment system guidelines.
