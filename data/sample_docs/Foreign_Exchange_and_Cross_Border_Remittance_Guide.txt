# APEX GLOBAL BANK — FOREIGN EXCHANGE & CROSS-BORDER REMITTANCE SOP (2026)

Version: v3.1 (Effective 2026)

## 1. LIBERALIZED REMITTANCE SCHEME (LRS)
Section 1.1 Permissible Limits & Eligibility:
- Resident Individual Annual Ceiling: Up to $250,000 USD (or equivalent in permitted foreign currencies) per financial year (April 1 to March 31).
- Eligible Purposes:
  1. Overseas education and student living expenses.
  2. Medical treatment abroad and attendant accommodation expenses.
  3. Private travel / tourist visits abroad (max $250,000 per FY).
  4. Gift or financial maintenance to close relatives residing overseas.
  5. Capital account transactions (foreign equity, mutual fund shares, immovable property acquisition).
- Prohibited Remittances: Remittances for trading in foreign exchange, lottery/gambling tickets, or purchase of FCCBs/prohibited commodities are strictly banned.

## 2. TAX COLLECTED AT SOURCE (TCS) RATES
Section 2.1 Applicable TCS Matrix (Finance Act Regulations):
- Overseas Education (funded through educational loan from approved bank):
  - Amount up to $8,500 (INR 7 Lakhs): 0% TCS.
  - Amount above $8,500 (INR 7 Lakhs): 0.50% TCS.
- Overseas Education & Medical Treatment (self-funded):
  - Amount up to $8,500: 0% TCS.
  - Amount above $8,500: 5.0% TCS.
- Overseas Tour Packages:
  - Up to $8,500: 5.0% TCS.
  - Above $8,500: 20.0% TCS.
- Other Remittances (Investments, Gifts, Real Estate, Offshore Maintenance):
  - Up to $8,500: 0% TCS.
  - Above $8,500: 20.0% TCS.

## 3. DOCUMENTATION & WIRE TRANSFER SLA
Section 3.1 Mandatory Outward Remittance Documents:
1. Form A2 with signed declaration under FEMA (Foreign Exchange Management Act).
2. Permanent Account Number (PAN Card) mandatory for all LRS transactions.
3. Form 15CA and Chartered Accountant Certificate 15CB for taxable overseas payments.
4. Valid invoice / university admission offer letter / medical diagnosis certificate.

Section 3.2 Settlement Protocols:
- Nostro Settlement via SWIFT MT103 / ISO 20022 messaging.
- Processing Turnaround Time: Outward remittances submitted before 13:00 local time are processed Same Day (T+0); later submissions are processed on the next business day (T+1).
