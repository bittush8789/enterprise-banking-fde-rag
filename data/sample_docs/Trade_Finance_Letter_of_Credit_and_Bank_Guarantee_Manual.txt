## 1. DOCUMENTARY LETTERS OF CREDIT (LC)
Section 1.1 Regulatory Standards & Types:
- All documentary credits issued by Apex Global Bank operate strictly under ICC Uniform Customs and Practice for Documentary Credits (UCP 600) and ISBP 745.
- Permissible LC Variants: Irrevocable Confirmed LC, Sight LC, Usance (Deferred Payment) LC, Transferable LC, Revolving LC, and Standby Letters of Credit (SBLC).

Section 1.2 Presentation Timelines & Examination SLA:
- Document Presentation Window: Beneficiary must present required shipping documents (Commercial Invoice, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, Insurance) within 21 calendar days from date of shipment, but within LC validity.
- Document Examination SLA: Trade Operations division must complete compliance examination and communicate discrepancy notices within 5 banking days of document receipt.
- Discrepancy Charges: $75.00 flat per discrepant presentation deducted from beneficiary proceeds.

## 2. BANK GUARANTEES (BG) & SURETY BONDS
Section 2.1 BG Classifications:
1. Financial Bank Guarantees: Issued in lieu of security deposits, earnest money (EMD), or loan repayment guarantees (Cash margin: 15% to 25%).
2. Performance Bank Guarantees: Issued in favor of project owners/government departments for fulfillment of contractual obligations (Cash margin: 10% to 20%).

Section 2.2 Invocation & Claim Period Mandate:
- Standard BG Validity: Specified contract period + mandatory 12-month claim lodgment period under Section 28 of the Indian Contract Act.
- Invocation Processing: When an unconditional invocation letter is received from the beneficiary prior to expiry of claim period, payment must be honored within 48 hours without raising commercial dispute defenses.

## 3. MARGIN STRUCTURE & COLLATERAL COVERAGE
- Baseline Cash Margin: 10% to 25% earmarked as Term Deposit with restrictive lien.
- 100% Margin Concession: 0.15% per quarter issuance fee if backed 100% by Apex Bank Term Deposit.
- Commission Schedule: 0.40% to 0.75% per quarter on non-deposit secured guarantees.
