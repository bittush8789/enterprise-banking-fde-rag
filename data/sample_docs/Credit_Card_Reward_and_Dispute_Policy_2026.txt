# APEX GLOBAL BANK — CREDIT CARD ISSUANCE, REWARDS & DISPUTE POLICY (2026)

Version: v3.2 (Effective 2026)

## 1. CREDIT CARD VARIANTS & ELIGIBILITY
Section 1.1 Card Variants & Annual Fees:
- Apex Titanium Card:
  - Annual Fee: $40 (Waived on annual spend >= $1,500).
  - Minimum Annual Income: $25,000 salaried.
  - Baseline APR: 3.50% per month (42.0% p.a.) on revolving unpaid balances.
- Apex Platinum Rewards Card:
  - Annual Fee: $100 (Waived on annual spend >= $3,500).
  - Minimum Annual Income: $60,000.
  - Complimentary Airport Lounge Access: 4 domestic visits per calendar quarter.
- Apex Infinite Metal Card:
  - Annual Fee: $350 (Non-waivable; includes $300 welcome travel voucher).
  - Minimum Annual Income: $150,000 or Total Relationship Value >= $100,000.
  - Unlimited Global Lounge Key access with 1 complimentary guest visit.

Section 1.2 Cash Advance & Over-Limit Charges:
- ATM Cash Advance Fee: 2.50% of withdrawn amount or minimum $10.00, whichever is higher. Interest accrues immediately from the transaction date (no grace period).
- Over-Limit Fee: 2.50% of the over-limit quantum, subject to a minimum charge of $15.00.

## 2. REWARD POINTS (RP) ACCRUAL & REDEMPTION
- Accrual Rate: 2 Reward Points per $1.00 spent on Titanium; 5 Reward Points per $1.00 spent on Platinum/Infinite.
- 10X Accelerated Reward Categories: International travel, dining, and registered online subscription services.
- Point Expiration: Reward points remain valid for 24 months from the month of accrual.
- Redemption Value:
  - Air Miles / Hotel Conversions: 1 Reward Point = $0.25 credit.
  - Statement Cash Credit: 1 Reward Point = $0.15 credit.
  - Merchandise / Gift Vouchers: 1 Reward Point = $0.20 credit.

## 3. BILLING CYCLES, GRACE PERIODS & LATE FEES
- Interest-Free Grace Period: Up to 50 days (applicable only if previous cycle statement balance was paid in full by due date).
- Minimum Amount Due (MAD): 5.0% of total statement balance + EMI installments + applicable government taxes.
- Late Payment Charges:
  - Statement balance up to $100: $0 (Grace fee exemption).
  - $101 to $500: $15.00 late fee.
  - $501 to $2,000: $30.00 late fee.
  - Greater than $2,000: $50.00 late fee.

## 4. CHARGEBACK & TRANSACTION DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Section 4.1 Dispute Reporting Timelines:
- Cardholders must notify the bank within 60 calendar days from the statement date regarding unauthorized transactions, merchant double-billing, or defective merchandise.
- Provisional Credit: Apex Bank provides provisional statement credit within 7 business days pending final card network arbitration (Visa/Mastercard/RuPay).
- Investigation Resolution SLA: Standard domestic chargebacks resolve within 45 days; complex international disputes resolve within 90 days.
