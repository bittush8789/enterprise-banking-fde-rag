# APEX GLOBAL BANK — INTERNAL IT & CYBERSECURITY OPERATIONAL STANDARD (2026)
Document ID: DOC-SOP-ITSEC-004
Classification: Restricted - Confidential Internal Operations
Department: Information Security, SOC & IT Infrastructure
Version: v1.9 (Effective 2026)
Authorized Personnel: System Administrators, Security Engineers, IT Managers, Risk Governance

---

## 1. ACCESS CONTROL & IDENTITY MANAGEMENT
Section 1.1 Password & Credential Standards:
- Minimum length: 14 alphanumeric characters including upper, lower, numbers, and special symbols.
- Password Expiration: Mandatory password rotation every 60 days.
- Account Lockout Policy: 5 consecutive failed login attempts trigger automated account lockout for 30 minutes.
- Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA): Hardware token (FIDO2) or Time-based One-Time Password (TOTP) is strictly mandatory for all Core Banking System (CBS) and administrative console logins.

Section 1.2 Principle of Least Privilege (PoLP):
- Access to production databases and customer financial records is governed by Role-Based Access Control (RBAC).
- Administrative access rights must undergo bi-annual access attestation reviews led by the Chief Information Security Officer (CISO).
- Shared or generic administrator accounts are strictly prohibited.

## 2. DATA LOSS PREVENTION (DLP) & PII SAFEGUARDING
Section 2.1 Customer Data Classification:
- Personally Identifiable Information (PII) including PAN numbers, Aadhaar numbers, card numbers, account numbers, and credit reports must be encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3.
- Unmasked display of full 16-digit Primary Account Numbers (PAN/Credit Card) in customer support portals is prohibited; only the last 4 digits may be visible.
- Exporting bulk customer financial records to unencrypted USB drives or external email domains is blocked automatically by endpoint DLP agents.

## 3. INCIDENT RESPONSE & BREACH NOTIFICATION
Section 3.1 Severity Levels:
- Severity 1 (Critical): Ransomware outbreak, active database exfiltration, unauthorized core wire routing disruption.
- Severity 2 (High): Compromise of privileged credentials, malware on perimeter jump hosts.
- Severity 3 (Medium): Phishing campaign targeting staff workstations, localized DDoS mitigation.

Section 3.2 Escalation Timelines:
- Critical incidents must be reported to the Head of SOC within 15 minutes of detection.
- Regulatory Notification: Mandatory notification to CERT-In and Central Bank Cyber Cell within 6 hours of incident confirmation.
