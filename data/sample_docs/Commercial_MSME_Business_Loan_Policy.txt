## 1. FACILITY TYPES & SCOPE
Apex Global Bank provides fund-based and non-fund-based credit lines to micro, small, and medium enterprises (MSME) and commercial enterprises:
1. Working Capital Limits: Cash Credit (CC) against hypothecation of stock/debtors, Overdraft (OD), and Export Credit.
2. Term Loans: Plant and machinery acquisition, factory land/building development, commercial vehicle loans.
3. Non-Fund Facilities: Letters of Credit (Inland & Foreign LC) and Bank Guarantees (Financial & Performance BG).

## 2. FINANCIAL RATIOS & UNDERWRITING BENCHMARKS
Section 2.1 Mandatory Financial Health Thresholds:
- Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR): Minimum benchmark DSCR is 1.25x across the loan tenure; ideal average DSCR >= 1.50x.
- Current Ratio (Liquidity): Minimum 1.33:1 (Current Assets to Current Liabilities).
- Total Outside Liabilities to Adjusted Tangible Net Worth (TOL/ATNW): Capped at maximum 4.0:1 for manufacturing; 3.5:1 for trading.
- Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR): Minimum benchmark >= 2.0x EBITDA.

Section 2.2 Collateral-Free MSME Credit (Credit Guarantee Scheme):
- Micro & Small Enterprises with annual revenue up to $5,000,000 qualify for collateral-free credit facilities up to $250,000 (INR 2.0 Crores) under the Central Credit Guarantee Trust (CGTMSE).
- Annual Guarantee Fee: 0.75% to 1.35% p.a. borne by the borrower based on risk grading.

## 3. APPRAISAL & DRAWING POWER (DP) CALCULATION
- Drawing Power on Paid Stock: Inventory valuation minus 25% mandatory borrower margin.
- Drawing Power on Book Debts: Eligible book debts up to 90 days from invoice date minus 30% margin. Receivables outstanding beyond 90 days are strictly excluded from DP.
- Monthly Stock Statements: Must be submitted by the 7th calendar day of each subsequent month. Failure to submit within 15 days results in a penal interest levy of 2.0% p.a. and temporary freeze of drawing power.

## 4. LOAN TENURE, MORATORIUM & SECURITY REQUIREMENTS
- Working Capital Review: Sanctioned for 12 months, subject to annual audited renewal.
- Term Loan Maximum Tenure: Up to 10 years (120 months) including a principal repayment moratorium of up to 12 months for greenfield industrial units.
- Primary Security: First pari-passu charge on all funded current assets and fixed assets.
- Collateral Security (for non-guaranteed facilities): Minimum 50% to 100% SARFAESI-compliant immovable commercial or residential property.
- Personal Guarantee: Unconditional personal guarantees of all directors, promoters, and majority equity shareholders holding > 10% equity.
