## 1. SAFE DEPOSIT LOCKER ALLOTMENT & OPERATION
Section 1.1 Locker Rent Matrix & Security Deposit:
- Small Locker: Annual rent $25.00 (INR 2,000)
- Medium Locker: Annual rent $50.00 (INR 4,000)
- Large Locker: Annual rent $100.00 (INR 8,000)
- Extra-Large Locker: Annual rent $200.00 (INR 15,000)
- Caution Deposit Term Deposit: At the time of locker allotment, the bank collects a Term Deposit covering 3 years of locker rent plus breaking-open charges ($100).

Section 1.2 Break-Open Procedures (Non-Operative Lockers):
- If locker rent remains unpaid for 3 consecutive years, or if locker remains unopened for 7 years despite notices:
  - Final 30-day registered notice dispatched to customer.
  - Break-open conducted in the presence of Branch Manager, 2 independent public witnesses, and a certified locksmith.
  - Inventory video-recorded and sealed in safe custody.

## 2. DECEASED DEPOSITOR CLAIM SETTLEMENT
Section 2.1 Accounts with Valid Nomination or Survivorship Mandate (Either or Survivor):
- Turnaround Time (SLA): Full settlement and disbursement within 15 calendar days from receipt of claim form, death certificate, and KYC of nominee/survivor.
- No Succession Certificate or Legal Heirship proof shall be insisted upon for registered nominee cases.

Section 2.2 Accounts WITHOUT Nomination:
- Threshold Limit up to $15,000 (INR 5,00,000):
  - Settled through Simplified Claim Procedure against Letter of Indemnity, Affidavit from legal heirs, and 2 independent sureties acceptable to the bank.
- Threshold Limit exceeding $15,000:
  - Requires Succession Certificate issued by a competent Civil Court, or Probated Will with Letter of Administration.
