# 📈 Business Case & Return on Investment (ROI) Analysis
**Project:** BankAssist AI Enterprise RAG  
**Prepared for:** Executive Leadership & COO, Apex Global Bank  
**Model Parameters:** 500 Employees | $45/hr Fully Burdened Cost | 12-Month Projection  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Implementing **BankAssist AI** across 500 branch and back-office banking staff yields an estimated **$1,080,000 in annual productivity savings**, achieving a **332% Return on Investment (ROI)** with a payback period of **2.8 months**.

## 3. Financial Cost Breakdown & Net ROI Calculation

### A. Investment & Operational Costs (Year 1):
- **Implementation & Engineering Consulting:** $180,000 (One-time)
- **Infrastructure (Docker, ChromaDB, Cloud Server):** $15,000 / year
- **LLM API Tokens (Groq Inference @ ~10,000 queries/day):** $24,000 / year
- **Ongoing Maintenance & Support:** $31,000 / year
- **Total Year 1 Cost:** **$250,000**

### B. Net Benefit & ROI:
$$\text{Net Annual Benefit} = \text{Productivity Savings} - \text{Year 1 Costs} = \$1,080,000 - \$250,000 = \mathbf{\$830,000}$$

$$\text{ROI} = \left( \frac{\text{Net Benefit}}{\text{Total Investment}} \right) \times 100 = \left( \frac{\$830,000}{\$250,000} \right) \times 100 = \mathbf{332\%}$$

$$\text{Payback Period} = \left( \frac{\$250,000}{\$1,080,000 / 12} \right) = \mathbf{2.78 \text{ Months}}$$
